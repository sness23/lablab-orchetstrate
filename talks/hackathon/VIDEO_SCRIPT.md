## Recording Tips

1. **Practice**: Run through 2-3 times before recording
2. **Pacing**: Speak slightly slower than normal
3. **Transitions**: Brief pause between sections
4. **Energy**: Stay enthusiastic but professional
5. **Demo**: If live demo fails, use screenshots
6. **Timer**: Keep visible, stay under 5:00

## Screen Recording Setup

- Resolution: 1920x1080
- Audio: Good microphone, quiet room
- Tool: OBS, Loom, or QuickTime
- Show: Slides + watsonx chat (if live demo)
