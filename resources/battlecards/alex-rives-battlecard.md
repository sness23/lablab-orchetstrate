## Technical Objection Handlers

### "We get good data at the synchrotron"

**Response**: "Absolutely - synchrotrons are excellent. The question is throughput. With ESM3 generating thousands of candidates, you need validation at that scale. On-site capability lets you screen quickly and reserve synchrotron time for the most challenging cases."

### "Our proteins don't crystallize well"

**Response**: "That's exactly why XtalCheck-S matters. Screen 96 conditions in-situ, find what works, then collect. For truly difficult cases, consider our XtaLAB Synergy-ED for electron diffraction on nanocrystals."

### "We're investing in cryo-EM"

**Response**: "Smart move for large complexes. But for the designed proteins and small molecule binding you're doing, X-ray crystallography gives you faster turnaround at 1/10th the cost. Most labs have both - they're complementary."
