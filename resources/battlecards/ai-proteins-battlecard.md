## Competitor Equipment to Watch

### Cryo-EM
- **Threat level**: Low for miniproteins
- **Counter**: Miniproteins are too small for cryo-EM (< 50 kDa)
- X-ray is the only option for their core platform

### NMR
- **Threat level**: Medium
- **Their pitch**: Solution structure, dynamics
- **Counter**:
  - NMR is slow and size-limited
  - Crystallography gives definitive atomic resolution
  - Complementary for dynamics studies

**Response**: "CROs work for early stage, but you're scaling up with the BMS deal. At 100+ structures needed for lead optimization, CRO costs exceed $500K and timelines become the bottleneck. On-site capability pays for itself in year one."

### "We don't have structural biology expertise"

**Response**: "That's where Rigaku's application scientists come in. We provide training, method development, and ongoing support. Many AI-native biotechs have successfully built crystallography capability with our help."

### "Capital is tight - we just raised Series A"

**Response**: "Your Series A and BMS deal give you runway for infrastructure. The question is ROI: $500K for equipment that accelerates your $400M partnership, or $500K+ per year to CROs with slower turnaround. The math favors ownership."
