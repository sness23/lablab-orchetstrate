# Rigaku X-ray Crystallography Systems

## Company Overview

Rigaku is a world leader in X-ray analytical instrumentation for structural biology. They provide solutions from crystallization to automated data collection at atomic resolution.

---

## Flagship Products for Protein Crystallography

### XtaLAB Synergy-S
**Best for: Research labs doing both protein and chemical crystallography**

#### Key Features
- **Kappa goniometer** with fast motor speeds
- **Telescopic two-theta arm** for flexible experiments
- **HPC hybrid photon counting detectors**: HyPix-6000HE, HyPix-Arc 100°, or HyPix-Arc 150°
- **PhotonJet-S microfocus X-ray sources** with variable divergence slits
- **Dual-source configuration**: Mo for chemical, Cu for protein crystallography

#### Why It's Ideal for AI-Generated Proteins
- High-throughput capability for validating many AI predictions
- Excellent for resolving large unit cells (common in novel proteins)
- Quick turnaround for iterative validation cycles

#### User Testimonial
> "I would recommend the XtaLAB Synergy-S system for structural biology labs because it is very useful for checking the diffraction quality of protein crystals and allows collection of high-quality data for well-diffracting protein crystals."

---

### XtalCheck-S
**Best for: High-throughput screening of crystallization plates**

#### Key Features
- Goniometer-mountable x,y,z stage
- Supports 96-well SBS format crystallization plates
- **In-situ screening** - analyze crystals without removing from plates
- User-friendly interface for rapid screening

#### Why It's Ideal for AI-Generated Proteins
- Screen hundreds of AI-generated protein variants quickly
- Identify best diffraction candidates before full data collection
- Reduces time wasted on poor-quality crystals

---

### XtaLAB Synergy-ED (Electron Diffraction)
**Best for: Nanocrystals and samples that don't form large crystals**

#### Key Features
- Collaboration with JEOL for electron diffraction
- Same control software as X-ray diffractometers
- Complete pipeline from selection to structure solution

#### Why It's Ideal for AI-Generated Proteins
- Many novel proteins may not crystallize well initially
- Electron diffraction works with much smaller crystals
- Alternative validation pathway when X-ray crystallography fails
