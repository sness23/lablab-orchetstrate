## Background

Stanford CS professor, MacArthur "Genius" Fellow (2004), co-founder of Coursera. Elected to National Academy of Sciences (2023). Named TIME 100 most influential people in AI (2024). Founded Insitro to apply machine learning to drug discovery at scale.

- **Technology**: Human cell models + machine learning
