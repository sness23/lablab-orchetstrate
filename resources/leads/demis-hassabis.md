# Lead Profile: Demis Hassabis

## Contact Information
- **Name**: Demis Hassabis
- **Title**: CEO & Co-founder
- **Location**: London, UK

## Background

Co-founded DeepMind in 2010, acquired by Google in 2014 for ~$500M. Led development of AlphaFold which solved the 50-year protein folding problem. **2024 Nobel Prize in Chemistry** for protein structure prediction. Founded Isomorphic Labs in 2021 for AI-driven drug discovery.

## Key Research: AlphaFold

### AlphaFold 2 (2020)
- Breakthrough accuracy in protein structure prediction
- Won CASP14 competition decisively
- Released 200M+ predicted structures

### AlphaFold 3 (2024)
- Predicts protein-ligand complexes
- Drug binding site prediction
- Protein-DNA/RNA interactions
