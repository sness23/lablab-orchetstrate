# Lead Profile: David Baker

## Contact Information
- **Name**: David Baker
- **Title**: Professor & Director, Institute for Protein Design
- **Location**: Seattle, WA
- **Twitter**: [@davidbaker_uwipd](https://x.com/davidbaker_uwipd)
- **Affiliations**: University of Washington, HHMI

## Background

Pioneer in computational protein design. Developed the Rosetta software suite used by thousands of researchers worldwide. **2024 Nobel Prize in Chemistry** for computational protein design. Founded multiple biotech companies including Cyrus Biotechnology.

## Key Research

### RoseTTAFold & RFdiffusion
- Deep learning for protein structure prediction
- Generative AI for de novo protein design
- Designs proteins that don't exist in nature

### Applications
- Designed enzymes for industrial processes
- Protein therapeutics and vaccines
- Novel protein materials

## Funding & Resources
