## Background

Alex Rives founded and led the Evolutionary Scale Modeling (ESM) project at Meta's fundamental AI research lab. After Meta disbanded the AI protein team in August 2023 during layoffs, he immediately founded EvolutionaryScale with his former colleagues.

## Key Research: ESM3

ESM3 is a groundbreaking generative AI model for biology:

- **Training**: 1 trillion teraflops on 2.78 billion proteins
- **Capability**: First model to simultaneously reason over sequence, structure, and function
- **Achievement**: Generated novel fluorescent protein equivalent to 500 million years of evolution
- **Applications**: Drug discovery, synthetic biology, protein design

## Equipment Needs Assessment

### Likely Equipment Requirements
1. **X-ray Crystallography** - For validating predicted structures

### Pain Points

- Require high-throughput screening of crystallization conditions
- Speed is critical - faster structure validation = faster iteration on models
