## Likely Objections & Responses

### "We have synchrotron access"

**Response**: "Synchrotrons are great for challenging cases. The question is throughput - if ESM3 generates 1000 candidates, can you validate enough to close the feedback loop? On-site capability handles the volume; synchrotron handles the edge cases."

### "We're focused on computational work"

**Response**: "Exactly - and experimental validation is what makes the computational work credible. Every novel structure you validate strengthens the training data for the next ESM version."

### "CZI/Biohub has equipment"

**Response**: "They do, but it's shared across many groups. Dedicated capability for EvolutionaryScale's specific needs ensures you're not waiting in queue when you need fast iteration."
