## Likely Objections & Responses

### "We're still early - not ready for equipment"

**Response**: "Understood. Now is actually the ideal time to plan your infrastructure. We can help you design the right setup for when you're ready, and there may be lead times to consider. No pressure on timing - let's make sure you have the information you need."

### "We'll start with CRO partners"

**Response**: "That's common for early stage. Worth considering: when you transition to in-house, having the right equipment from day one avoids the learning curve of switching systems. We can help you plan for that transition."

### "We may focus on cryo-EM"

**Response**: "Cryo-EM is powerful for certain applications. For the rapid iteration cycles of AI-driven design, X-ray crystallography often provides faster turnaround at lower cost. Most groups find they need both - happy to discuss how they complement each other."
